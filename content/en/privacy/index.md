## Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

### Information We Collect

SelfVPS Guide (the "Site") values your privacy. The information we collect is minimal:

- **Analytics Data**: We use [Umami](https://umami.is/) for anonymous visit statistics — no personally identifiable information is collected, and no tracking cookies are set.
- **Comment Data**: If you use the comment feature, the nickname and email address you provide are used only for display and reply notifications.
- **Subscription Email**: If you subscribe to our Newsletter, we only store the email address you provide for sending update notifications.

### What We Don't Do

- ❌ No third-party ad trackers
- ❌ No cross-site tracking cookies
- ❌ We do not sell your personal information to anyone
- ❌ No embedded social tracking code from Facebook, Twitter, etc.
