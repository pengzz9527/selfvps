## About SelfVPS Guide

**SelfVPS Guide** is a technical tutorial site focused on **VPS self-hosting** and **cloud cost savings**.

### Our Mission

As cloud computing costs continue to rise and vendor lock-in becomes increasingly concerning, more individual developers and small businesses are seeking cost-effective, controllable alternatives. SelfVPS Guide aims to:

- 📖 Provide clear, practical **VPS self-hosting tutorials**
- 💰 Share **cloud cost-saving strategies** and value analysis
- 🛠️ Introduce excellent **open-source projects** and their deployment methods
- 🔒 Emphasize the importance of **data privacy** and **self-sovereignty**

### About the Author

**Ross Zhang** — Full-stack developer and open-source enthusiast. Years of experience in VPS operations and cloud architecture, dedicated to helping people gain a better internet experience through self-hosting.
