## Money-Saving Tips

### 1. Choose the Right Billing Cycle

Most providers offer monthly or yearly discounts:
- **Hetzner**: Hourly billing, flexible
- **Vultr**: Hourly billing, start/stop anytime
- **DigitalOcean**: Monthly, occasional annual deals

### 2. Use Free Credits

- **Google Cloud**: $300 free credit for new users (90 days)
- **Oracle Cloud**: Always-free tier (1 vCPU/1GB ARM)
- **AWS**: 12-month free tier (750 hours t2.micro/month)

### 3. Monitor Resource Usage

Don't pay for a high-end VPS if you only use a fraction. Use `htop`, `df -h`, and other tools to monitor usage and adjust accordingly.

## Summary

When choosing a VPS, look beyond price. Consider performance, network quality, datacenter location, and support. Start with a low-cost plan and scale up as needed.

> 💡 **Tip**: Many providers offer free trials. Test actual network latency and performance before committing.
